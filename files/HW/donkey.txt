### The Little Donkey

A little ##### walked one day,  
Along the road in his own way.  
With floppy ears and a gentle face,  
He carried loads from place to place.

He worked all day beneath the sun,  
But still he thought of having fun.  
He loved the grass, the sky so blue,  
And muddy puddles to walk through.

People laughed and called him slow,  
But he kept walking, row by row.  
He never gave up, he did his best,  
And always worked without a rest.

So never judge him by his pace,  
For every creature has its place.  
The little ##### taught us too:  
**Keep moving on, and believe in you!** 🫏
